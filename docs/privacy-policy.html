Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 12, 2026

Detour ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Detour mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

When you use Detour, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: name, username, date of birth, gender, and profile photos you provide during registration.
  • Profile information: interests, lifestyle preferences, dating preferences, Instagram handle, and travel plans you choose to share.
  • Location data: your current city and country (via device GPS) to show you nearby users and relevant content. Location is only accessed when the app is in use and only if you have granted permission.
  • Camera and photo library: accessed only when you choose to upload profile photos or photos for help requests. We do not access your camera or photos without your explicit action.
  • Messages: content of messages you send to other users through the app.
  • Usage data: interactions within the app such as swipes, matches, and profile views.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To show you relevant profiles based on your location and preferences
  • To facilitate matches and messaging between users
  • To display your profile to other users
  • To send push notifications about matches, messages, and app updates
  • To improve and maintain the app

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Clerk for authentication (phone, Google, Apple sign-in)
  • Convex for data storage and real-time functionality
  • RevenueCat for subscription management
  • Expo for push notifications and app updates
  • Google Play for payment processing

Each of these services has their own privacy policy governing how they handle your data.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Convex cloud infrastructure. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Authentication is handled through Clerk with encrypted connections.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your profile information is visible to other Detour users as part of the app's core functionality. We may share data with third-party services listed above solely to operate the app.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Edit or update your profile information at any time
  • Delete your account through the app settings
  • Block and report other users
  • Control location permissions through your device settings
  • Control camera and photo library permissions through your device settings

Children's Privacy

Detour is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
